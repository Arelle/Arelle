Arelle is an open source platform for validating and processing XBRL. First
created in 2010, it makes XBRL data more accessible through a desktop
application, command line interface, Python API, Docker image and web service.

## Mission

Arelle's mission is to support the global adoption of XBRL by giving regulators,
software providers, auditors and filers a free, standards-based platform for
working with structured business data.

## Stewardship

Arelle is released under the [Apache License 2.0][apache-license] and maintained
in the open by the Arelle team and community contributors. Workiva holds the
project's copyright.

## Get Involved

Ask questions and discuss Arelle in the [Google Group][google-group], take the
first steps towards becoming a contributor by reading the project's
[contributor guide][contributor-guide], or email
[support@arelle.org][support-email] for an invitation to our public monthly
Zoom standup meeting where we discuss what's new and what we're currently
working on.
