This month’s Arelle updates include a variety of improvements to both the core Arelle platform and the Arelle iXBRL Viewer.

The Arelle 2.30.14 release includes support for the EDGAR 24.2 taxonomy, enhancements to the Session Python API, and the addition of more Danish Business Authority validation rules.

The Arelle iXBRL Viewer 1.4.30 release includes several new capabilities:

- Calc 1.1 relationships now display directly in the viewer
- Webserver support has been added
- New support for vendor MMS Solutions AG, enhancing multi-vendor compatibility

To get involved with future work, please join the Arelle team monthly standup on the fourth Tuesday of each month at 1400 UTC. Please [contact us][support-email] for meeting details.
