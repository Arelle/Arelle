February has been a highly productive month of refinement and acceleration for the Arelle project. With the release of **Arelle 2.38.17** and **iXBRL Viewer 1.4.88**, we have delivered major performance optimizations, clearer project guidelines, and updated regulatory previews.

Here is what we accomplished this month:

## Performance & Rendering Enhancements

- **Faster Load Times:** We have optimized loading speeds, yielding a **15% load time improvement for US-GAAP** taxonomies.
- **Table Linkbase:** This update addresses and resolves critical **Table Linkbase rendering bug fixes**.

## Validation & Compliance

- **EDGAR 26.1 Previews:** Early-stage support is now live with **EDGAR 26.1 preview builds**.
- **Rule Improvements:** We have made targeted **DBA validation rule improvements** and **KVK validation rule improvements** to strengthen our Danish and Dutch reporting support.

## Infrastructure & Policy Updates

- **Official Versioning Policy:** To provide more clarity and transparency around our releases, we have published our official [Versioning Policy][versioning-policy].
- **New Conformance Suite Runner:** We have transitioned to a modernized testing setup. You can view the code and progress at our [new conformance suite runner repository][conformance-suite-runner].

## Arelle iXBRL Viewer (v1.4.88)

- **Visual Fixes:** This release resolves an interface bug, providing a **fix for column layout fact highlight wrapping** to keep report presentation clean and readable.

To get involved with future work, please join the Arelle team monthly standup on the fourth Tuesday of each month at 1400 UTC. Please [contact us][support-email] for meeting details.
