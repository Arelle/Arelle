September was another active month of development for the Arelle project, with updates rolling out across versions **2.37.50 through 2.37.60**. This cycle brings a healthy mix of platform usability enhancements, diagnostic upgrades, and crucial regulatory updates.

Here is a breakdown of what’s new:

- **Regulatory & Validation Enhancements**: This release introduces preview builds for **EDGAR 25.3** and adds further **Japanese EDINET validation rules**. We have also made key **LEI validation improvements**.
- **Usability & GUI Updates**: To streamline your workflow, you can now **load EDINET manifests directly from the GUI**. We’ve also **improved plugin discovery** to make managing extensions a smoother experience.
- **Logging & Diagnostics**: Developers and power users will benefit from **improved IXDS model reference logging**, making troubleshooting much more straightforward.

To get involved with future work, please join the Arelle team monthly standup on the fourth Tuesday of each month at 1400 UTC. Please [contact us][support-email] for meeting details.
