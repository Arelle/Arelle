---
title: May 2025 Update
date: 2025-05-30T13:10:00
---

This month, the Arelle team added support for EDGAR 25.2 preview builds and expanded validation coverage, including additional DBA and NL rules. Report package validation was also improved, and users can now save iXDS reports to OIM formats (JSON/CSV) directly from the GUI.

The iXBRL Viewer introduced better dark mode handling across multiple documents and tabs, along with support for Node.js 24.

To get involved with future work, please join the Arelle team monthly standup on the fourth Tuesday of each month at 1400 UTC. Please [contact us][support-email] for meeting details.
