November brings the release of **Arelle 2.37.72** alongside **iXBRL Viewer 1.4.80**. This month, the development focus has shifted toward laying the groundwork for 2025 regulatory reporting standards, expanding international validation suites, and optimizing our delivery packages.

## Platform & Validation Milestones

- **ESEF 2025 Preview:** We have introduced work-in-progress (WIP) support for the **ESEF 2025 disclosure system**. This is currently incomplete but serves as an early foundation for upcoming filings.
- **EDINET Rules Milestone:** We added more validation rules, bringing the total to **401 EDINET validation rules** (now approximately **95% complete**).
- **Netherlands iXBRL:** Updated the platform to support **Netherlands iXBRL 2025 validation** requirements.
- **Command Line Improvement:** A new --optionsFile argument has been introduced, allowing you to **load CLI options directly from a JSON file** for cleaner automation.

## iXBRL Viewer Updates (v1.4.80)

- **Optimized Package Size:** We have successfully reduced the footprint of both our **Python and npm packages**, ensuring a lighter and faster installation process.
